---
title: Chocolate Muffins
description: Moist muffins with cocoa and chocolate chips. No eggs, no butter.
category: baking
order: 20
servings: "12 muffins"
time: 35 min
ingredients:
  - 300 g flour (spelt or plain wheat)
  - 50 g cocoa powder
  - 120 g sugar
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 1 pinch of salt
  - 120 ml neutral vegetable oil
  - 350 ml oat milk
  - 100 g dark chocolate chips
tip: Drop a few raspberries into each case before the batter goes in. Even better.
---

1. Preheat the oven to 180 °C (160 °C fan) and line a muffin tin with paper cases.
2. Mix the flour, cocoa, sugar, salt and baking powder in a bowl.
3. Gradually pour in the oil and the oat milk and stir briefly until smooth.
4. Set aside a tablespoon of chocolate chips and fold the rest into the batter.
5. Fill the cases and scatter the remaining chips on top.
6. Bake for about 20 minutes.
