---
title: Mushroom Burgers
description: Homemade patties from mushrooms, oats and chickpea flour, with burger sauce and all the trimmings.
category: main
order: 3
servings: "10 patties"
time: 60 min
ingredients:
  - "Patties:"
  - 600 g button mushrooms
  - 3 onions
  - 2 cloves of garlic
  - 75 g chickpea flour plus 150 ml water
  - 25 g parsley
  - 150 g fine rolled oats
  - 4 tbsp mustard
  - 3 tsp sweet paprika
  - Salt, pepper, oil
  - "Sauce:"
  - 6 tbsp ketchup
  - 3 tbsp vegan mayo
  - 1 tbsp vegan crème fraîche
  - 1 tsp mustard
  - ½ tsp sugar (optional)
  - Salt, pepper, paprika
  - "To serve:"
  - Burger buns
  - Lettuce, tomatoes, gherkins, crispy fried onions
tip: Potato wedges go perfectly with these. Shake them hard with salt and paprika.
---

1. Dice the mushrooms, onions and garlic.
2. Heat oil in a large pan and fry the mushrooms and onions over high heat for 5 minutes. Add the garlic after 4 minutes.
3. Whisk the chickpea flour with the water and set aside.
4. In a bowl, combine the oats, mustard, parsley, the mushroom mix and the chickpea mixture. Season with salt, paprika and pepper.
5. Shape into patties. If the mix doesn’t hold together, add a little more chickpea flour.
6. Fry the patties in oil over medium heat, ideally with a lid on. Add a slice of vegan cheese after flipping if you like.
7. For the sauce, stir all the ingredients together.
8. Warm the buns briefly in the oven and build your burger however you like.
