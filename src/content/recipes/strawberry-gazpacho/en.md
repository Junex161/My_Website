---
title: Strawberry and Pepper Gazpacho
description: A cold summer soup of strawberries, cucumber and peppers, with crisp baguette for dipping.
category: soup
order: 10
servings: "4"
time: 20 min
ingredients:
  - 500–750 g strawberries
  - 1 cucumber
  - 2 bell peppers
  - Salt, pepper, herbes de Provence
  - 1 baguette
  - Olive oil
  - "Optional: balsamic glaze"
---

1. Chop the strawberries, cucumber and peppers and blend until smooth.
2. Season with salt, pepper and herbes de Provence.
3. Slice the baguette and fry the slices in olive oil until golden.
4. Add a little balsamic to the gazpacho if you like, dip the bread in and enjoy.
