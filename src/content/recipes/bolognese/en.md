---
title: Vegan Bolognese
description: The classic, with plant-based mince, a splash of wine and plenty of patience while it simmers.
category: main
order: 4
servings: "4"
time: 45 min
ingredients:
  - 500 g linguine or spaghetti
  - 1 l passata
  - 1 pack of vegan mince (not the dry kind)
  - 2 onions
  - Olive oil
  - A splash of wine
  - Italian herbs, herbes de Provence
  - Sweet paprika, cayenne, salt, pepper
  - "Optional: mushrooms, courgette, other vegetables"
  - "Optional: a bay leaf"
---

1. Dice the onions and soften them in oil.
2. Cook the pasta in salted water.
3. Add the mince to the onions and brown it a little.
4. Add vegetables if you like.
5. Deglaze with a splash of wine.
6. Pour in the passata.
7. Season generously and let it simmer.
8. Maybe lay a bay leaf on top, purely for the looks.
