---
title: Potato and Broccoli Gratin
description: Potatoes and broccoli in a creamy plant-milk sauce, baked until golden.
category: main
order: 5
servings: "3–4"
time: 50 min
ingredients:
  - 600 g potatoes
  - 400 g broccoli
  - 1 red onion
  - 1 tbsp flour
  - 300 ml plant milk
  - 200 ml vegan whipping cream
  - A little oil
  - Salt and pepper
---

1. Boil the peeled potatoes and the broccoli until just tender.
2. Slice the potatoes, layer them in a baking dish and scatter the broccoli florets on top.
3. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
4. Heat oil in a pot and soften the diced onion. Dust with the flour and cook briefly.
5. Stir in the plant milk first, then the cream bit by bit. Let it thicken and season with salt and pepper.
6. Pour the sauce evenly over the vegetables.
7. Bake for about 20 minutes until golden on top.
