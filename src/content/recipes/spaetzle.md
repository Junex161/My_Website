---
title: Vegan Spätzle
description: Swabian egg noodles without the eggs. Soy milk and a bit of turmeric do the job.
category: main
servings: "4"
time: "40 min"
ingredients:
  - 100 g flour
  - 500 g spätzle flour
  - 80–100 g soy flour
  - 600 ml soy milk
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - 2 tsp salt
  - ½ tsp herb salt
  - Nutmeg
  - Pepper
  - Oil for the pan
---

1. Mix the flours.
2. Add nutmeg, salt, herb salt and turmeric.
3. Work everything into a smooth batter with the soy milk, season with pepper, and let it rest covered for 10 minutes.
4. Bring a pot of salted water to the boil.
5. Press some batter into the water with a spätzle slicer or press.
6. Lift the spätzle out as soon as they float to the surface.
7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 until the batter is used up.
8. Toss the finished spätzle in hot oil in a pan.
