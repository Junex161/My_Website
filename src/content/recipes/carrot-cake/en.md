---
title: Carrot Cake
description: A moist carrot cake with ground almonds and apple sauce, topped with a cream-cheese frosting.
category: baking
order: 21
servings: "1 springform tin"
time: 1 h 20 min
ingredients:
  - "Cake:"
  - 450 g carrots
  - 170 g neutral oil
  - 150 g apple sauce
  - 250 g plain flour
  - 200 g ground almonds (or half hazelnuts)
  - 160 g sugar
  - 2 tsp baking powder
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  - ½ tsp cinnamon (optional)
  - ½ tsp salt
  - Vegan margarine for the tin
  - "Frosting:"
  - 150 g vegan cream cheese
  - 40 g icing sugar
  - 1 sachet vanilla sugar
  - 1–2 tsp lemon juice
tip: Top it with little marzipan carrots and it looks like it came from a bakery.
---

1. Peel and finely grate the carrots.
2. In a large bowl, stir together the oil and apple sauce, then add the carrots.
3. Add the flour, almonds, baking powder, baking soda, vinegar, sugar, cinnamon and salt and mix thoroughly.
4. Line a springform tin with baking paper, grease the sides with margarine and pour in the batter.
5. Bake in a preheated oven at 200 °C for at least 45 minutes, more likely an hour. Check with a skewer.
6. Let it cool in the tin first, then completely on a rack.
7. For the frosting, stir together the cream cheese, icing sugar, vanilla sugar and lemon juice, then spread it over the cake.
