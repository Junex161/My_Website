---
title: Spätzle
description: Swabian egg noodles without the eggs. Soy milk and a pinch of turmeric do the job.
category: main
order: 1
servings: "4"
time: 40 min
ingredients:
  - 100 g plain flour
  - 500 g spätzle flour (or durum wheat semolina flour)
  - 80–100 g soy flour
  - 600 ml soy milk
  - 1 tsp turmeric
  - 2 tsp salt
  - ½ tsp herb salt
  - Nutmeg
  - Pepper
  - Oil for the pan
tip: No spätzle maker? Scrape the batter off a wet board with a knife. They'll just look a bit more rustic.
---

1. Mix the flours.
2. Add nutmeg, salt, herb salt and turmeric.
3. Work everything into a smooth batter with the soy milk, season with pepper and let it rest, covered, for 10 minutes.
4. Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil.
5. Press some of the batter into the water with a spätzle maker or press.
6. Lift the spätzle out as soon as they float to the surface.
7. Repeat steps 5 and 6 until the batter is used up.
8. Toss the finished spätzle in hot oil in a pan.
