---
title: Sweet Potato Curry
description: A creamy curry with sweet potatoes, tomatoes and coconut milk. Quick to make, keeps you full for ages.
category: main
order: 2
servings: "3–4"
time: 35 min
ingredients:
  - Jasmine or basmati rice
  - 2–3 onions
  - 2–3 cloves of garlic
  - 1–2 sweet potatoes
  - 1 tin of chopped tomatoes
  - 1 tin of coconut milk
  - Oil
  - Curry powder
  - Salt and pepper
  - "Optional: coriander seeds, ginger"
  - "Optional: naan or flatbread"
---

1. Dice the sweet potatoes and onions as small as you can and chop the garlic.
2. Cook the rice in salted water according to the packet.
3. Soften the onions in a pot with rapeseed oil, then add the garlic after a while.
4. Add the sweet potato and fry it briefly.
5. Pour in the tomatoes and the coconut milk.
6. Simmer until the sweet potatoes are soft.
7. Season with curry powder, salt, pepper and whatever else you like.
8. Serve with the rice. Et voilà!
